**Glide through the air with style using a colorful umbrella!**

## ✨ Features

- **Open/Close Mechanic**: Right-click to open or close your umbrella
- **Slow Fall**: When falling with an open umbrella, your descent is slowed by 20%
- **No Fall Damage**: The umbrella resets your fall distance while gliding
- **Beautiful Particles**:
  - ☁️ Cloud particles spawn when opening the umbrella
  - ⚪ White ash particles appear while gliding
- **Sound Effects**: Satisfying wool sounds when opening/closing
- **Durability System**: 128 uses before breaking
- **Easy Repair**: Repair with **ANY dye** or **ANY colored carpet**!
- **Enchantable**: Can be enchanted with Unbreaking, Mending, and more

##  How to Use

1. Hold the umbrella in your main hand or offhand
2. **Right-click** to open it
3. Jump or fall from a height - the umbrella will automatically slow your descent!
4. **Right-click** again to close it when you're done

##  Technical Details

- **Durability**: 128 uses
- **Repair Items**: All dyes + all carpet colors (16 types!)
- **Enchantment Value**: 1 (standard)
- **Particles**: Server-side for optimal performance
- **Compatibility**: Works in singleplayer and multiplayer
